# Surface: index.html (single-page marketing site)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: local St Marys/Western Sydney residents discovering the business via mobile search or Google Maps, deciding in seconds whether to call or drive over. Job: confirm legitimacy and service fit, then convert to a phone call, email, or visit. Proof/content: verified business facts from PRODUCT.md (address, hours, services, 5.0★/8-review TrueLocal rating), licensed garment-care photography standing in for real shop photos. Constraints: no backend, no booking, no invented claims/pricing/years-in-business/certifications.

What must remain untouched: the user's own pinned specifics — exact palette hex values, Playfair Display/Cormorant Garamond + Inter/Manrope type pairing, section order (nav → hero → trust → services → process → why-us → gallery → video → reviews → contact → FAQ → final CTA → footer), hero copy ("Care That Keeps Your Clothes Looking Their Best."), and the explicit ban on generic templated patterns.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page doesn't just photograph garment care, it performs the transformation it sells — the visitor's scroll re-enacts a garment's own journey from worn to restored. This refuses the category default of a static photo-hero bolted onto an otherwise generic light page.

OWN-WORLD: A single continuous tonal journey, not a flat palette. The hero opens in a dim, warm-lit "before" register (near-black charcoal ground, a single warm key light, steam and fabric texture doing all the work) and the page resolves, section by section, into the brief's pinned Warm Ivory/Champagne Beige "after" register by the trust section — dawn breaking, echoed nowhere else in literal copy, just felt in the ground color. Hairline seam-stitch dividers (a literal dry-cleaning/tailoring motif, thread-thin, Champagne Beige) mark every section boundary instead of shadows or cards. Interactive elements (buttons, service panels, gallery tiles) carry a restrained foil-like sheen on hover/focus — a single soft diagonal highlight sweep — standing in for "pressed and finished" rather than a generic shadow-lift. The garment-care-process section uses oversized numerals (01–04) as the dominant visual weight with minimal connecting hairlines, not icon tiles. A persistent, fixed-position call affordance (small, always in the same corner past the hero) is the one UI element that never moves, the way a trusted instrument reads the same place every time.

STORY: A visitor arrives unsure this is more than a generic laundromat; within one viewport the cinematic hero proves it's a serious garment-care craft business; scrolling down, the tone visibly brightens as trust, services, process, and real (sourced) social proof accumulate; by the final CTA they're in bright, confident ivory and forest-green, primed to tap call or email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed dim cinematic garment/steam image or video, deep charcoal vignette at the edges. Left-aligned column: small tracked eyebrow "ST MARYS VILLAGE DRY CLEANERS", large serif headline "Care That Keeps Your Clothes Looking Their Best.", one line of support copy, two CTAs (Get in Touch / Explore Our Services) with the foil-hover treatment, subtle scroll cue. Transparent nav bar over the top third, going solid ivory with blur after ~80px scroll.

FORM: Grounded direction #3 of 7 (ordered by resonance from the business's own world: 1-claim-ticket ephemera, 2-couture garment label, **3-pressed-garment steam/transformation photography [assigned & built]**, 4-haberdashery shopfront signage, 5-garment-rail rhythm, 6-tailor's pattern-paper, 7-bridal preservation box). This is also the exact world the user's own brief already specifies (steam, pressed fabric, cinematic garment photography), so the roll confirms rather than overrides the brief. seed: 085d4863.

Raises absorbed from declined challengers (each is a discipline, not a costume): visible hairline "seam" dividers in place of shadows/cards (from the Rietveld sliding-house's honest structural joints); a soft foil-sheen micro-highlight on interactive elements (from the matchbook drawer's foil-stamped covers); a literal dark-to-light tonal arc down the page (from the theatrical dawn cyclorama); oversized wordless-arrow-style step numerals for the process section (from the brick-instruction-book's exploded steps); one fixed-position, never-relocated call affordance (from the aircraft instrument six-pack's trusted, constant reading position).

Declined without adoption: Cyworld minihompy isometric room (no material fit for a trust-driven local service brand).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
