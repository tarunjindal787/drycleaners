# Surface: index.html (single-page marketing site)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: local St Marys/Western Sydney residents discovering the business via mobile search or Google Maps, deciding in seconds whether to call or drive over. Job: confirm legitimacy and service fit, then convert to a phone call, email, or visit. Proof/content: verified business facts from PRODUCT.md (address, hours, services, 5.0★/8-review TrueLocal rating, same-day/24-hour turnaround), real photography already hosted in `assets/`. Constraints: no backend, no online booking, no pickup/delivery (the business is walk-in/drive-up only), no invented claims/pricing/years-in-business/certifications/testimonials.

What must remain untouched: real business facts and links (phone, email, address, hours, TrueLocal rating/link, services list, Google Maps embed), the current section order (nav → hero → services → process → why-us/stats → gallery → contact/location → footer), and every working accessible behavior (mobile drawer, scroll reveal, gallery lightbox with keyboard/swipe support, fixed call affordance). The real four-step process is drop-off-to-collection in person, never pickup/delivery.

Superseded: this surface has shipped two prior systems — "The Lit Counter" (amber/charcoal, glassmorphism, bento cards) and "The Linen Press" (forest-green/cream/peach/sage editorial, DM Serif Display + Manrope, image-led tiles). Both are fully replaced by "Quiet Luxury": an ivory/charcoal/champagne minimalist identity (Playfair Display + Inter), explicitly requested as a visually-extreme, text-minimized, Apple/Aesop/luxury-fashion-editorial redesign. The user confirmed two scoped conflicts before this build: (1) the hero keeps its existing custom video asset (restyled) rather than being replaced with a fabricated "3D garment render" that cannot honestly be produced without a 3D asset pipeline; (2) all motion/interaction (card tilt, parallax, scroll reveal) is implemented as lightweight CSS 3D + vanilla JS rather than adding Three.js/GSAP as real dependencies, to keep the static no-build-step architecture and avoid an empty-looking WebGL canvas with no real 3D assets.

This redesign also deliberately folded or removed two previously-standalone sections per the brief's "minimize text, maximize design" mandate: the dedicated Reviews section (its one sourced fact, the 5.0 TrueLocal rating, now lives in the Why Choose Us stat row and a single reviews-link) and the FAQ accordion (absent from the brief's exhaustive section list and the single most text-dense component on the page; its load-bearing facts — hours, location, "no listed prices" — remain covered by Contact and Why Choose Us). Nothing functional was lost: no interactive capability was removed, only prose.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A premium dry-cleaning brand should read like a boutique lifestyle label, not a local-service template, so the page replaces descriptive paragraphs with large typography, real photography, and asymmetric editorial composition — visual-first throughout, minimal copy everywhere.

OWN-WORLD: Warm ivory and pure white (#F5F2EA / #FFFFFF) carry the resting registers (services, gallery, contact card); deep charcoal (#171918) anchors the dark registers (hero, why-us, contact CTA panel, footer); muted champagne (#C7AD85, with a darkened #7D6342 text-safe variant) is the sole accent, used only for small labels, numerals, and CTA chips, never a filled surface at scale; soft stone (#DCD7CD) is a quiet divider/neutral. Playfair Display (true bold, used large) pairs with Inter (clean, restrained body/label text) — a deliberately different serif voice and a first-time true-bold headline treatment, distinguishing this from both superseded systems. Radius is a deliberate contrast: images and tiles are nearly square (2px), while every button is a full pill — soft human controls against sharp editorial imagery. Services and gallery both use an asymmetric editorial mosaic built from independent, explicitly fixed-height flex row-groups (never CSS Grid row-spanning with auto-sized tracks) so varied tile widths never produce the uneven-row-height/empty-space bug this project hit twice before with grid-based "bento" layouts.

STORY: A visitor lands on a full-bleed dark hero — the business's own commercial video, a single oversized "CARE, REDEFINED." headline, one short line, two calm CTAs — and knows immediately this is a considered, premium brand. Scrolling down, services appear as a handful of large photographs with a title each, no paragraphs; the four-step process is reduced to an icon, a numeral, and one word per step; Why Choose Us states its proof as four large stats (5.0 rating, 24hr turnaround, drive-up access, locally run) divided by hairlines, not sentences; the gallery is a quiet asymmetric grid of real garment-care photography; the page closes on a dark split-screen contact moment ("YOUR GARMENTS. OUR CARE.") pairing one clear call-to-action with a real address, hours and map. Every section communicates in seconds, not paragraphs.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A floating, fully transparent pill navbar (wordmark "ST MARYS VILLAGE / DRY CLEANERS" left, Services/Our Care/Contact center, a champagne "Call Now" pill right) that gains an ivory blur-glass fill on scroll. Full-bleed hero below it: the existing hero video, darkened with a charcoal scrim, a massive centered Playfair Display headline "CARE, / REDEFINED." (second line in champagne), one short supporting line, two centered CTAs, a subtle mouse-parallax on the text block and a scroll cue.

FORM: User-pinned direction via an extremely detailed, fully specified master prompt (named hex palette, typography pairing, section-by-section visual spec, an explicit "remove ~70% of copy" mandate, and a strict do/don't list) — not rolled via `concept-seed`. Two real technical/asset conflicts were surfaced and resolved with the user via structured questions before build (hero-asset fate; animation tech stack), both documented above under Superseded. Full replace of both prior systems was confirmed by the brief's own exhaustiveness and repeated emphasis, consistent with this project's established pattern of treating a complete, prescriptive master prompt as having already decided the "full replace vs. refine" question.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with DESIGN.md rewritten for Quiet Luxury, a mechanical detector pass (impeccable detect), and real-browser verification (overflow, console errors, image loading, lightbox/nav interaction) across 1920 down to 375px before shipping.
