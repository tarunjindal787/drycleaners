From Drop-Off to Collection

Looking after your wardrobe should feel effortless. Here's exactly what happens to your garment once it's in our hands.

01

Drop Off

Pull up at The Village Centre and bring your garments in — drive-up access right outside the main entrance, no appointment needed.

02

Care That Starts With Listening

No two garments are quite the same. We'll take the time to understand what each piece needs, discuss any particular concerns, and help you choose the right care.

03

Handled With Attention

Behind every refreshed garment is careful handling and an eye for detail — cleaned, treated and pressed properly, by hand where it matters.

04

Ready for Collection

Same-day or 24-hour turnaround, with an SMS the moment your garment is ready to collect.

05

Collect, Refreshed

Come back, pick it up, and you're ready to wear it — simple as that.
